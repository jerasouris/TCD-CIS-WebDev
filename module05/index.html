Dandys World

Overview:

Dandys World is a mascot-horror survival game made by the blushcrunch team on roblox. In this game you play as characters called Toons, each with unique roles and stats. Your goal is to survive endless levels (floors) by doing machines to open the elevators while avoiding hostile enemy versions of Toons known as Twisteds.

I enjoy this game because...

The grinding aspect, along with the accomplishing feel of making it to high floors. With that I also very much enjoy the play style of being certain supports and extractors. As distracting for too long definitely becomes stressful as the rare and main twisteds spawn rate goes up. though either way its a fun game and i enjoy playing it with my friends for hours.

The Basics

common things you will need to know about well playing:

toons

Toons are the characters you play as. At the start, you can choose either Poppy or Boxton. After that, you'll need to collect ichor and research to unlock more Toons.
Each Toon has a passive ability, and or active ability, some have both. Non-main Toons have 15-star stats, while main Toons have 16-star stats. Main Toons have better stats and abilities because they’re canonly the main characters of this cartoon in game. With that normal toons have 3 hearts and main toons have 2 hearts.

twisteds

On every floor, you face Twisteds, starting with two. The number of Twisteds increases depending on how many teammates are still alive. The maximum is 8, and you will not see duplicates of the same Twisted.
There are passive and aggressive Twisteds. Some have abilities, while others don’t. For example, Razzle and Dazzle activate if you enter their circle and can hit you, while Astro gives you a debuff that reduces your stam regeneration when seen by him.

machines & events

To clear each floor, you must extract all of the machines. There are three types of machines. Treadmills, extraction based on your toons speed stat. Circles & Regulars extraction speed is based on your toons extraction stat. Each machine has a skill check which gives a bit more progress to the machine.
Floors also have events. Blackouts reduce visibility. Ichor Leaks puddles cause slowness and an Illness debuff (slower extraction/harder skill checks). Ice Floors (Seasonal) makes the floors slippery. Springfever (Seasonal) speed boost for toons and twisteds, but Stamina regenerates much slower and twisteds lose you much faster.

trinkets

Trinkets are helpful in all sorts of ways if you pick the right ones. there's trinkets for speed, stam, extraction, cooldown, skill check, and misc. It’s in your best practice to use trinkets for stats your toon lacks in. Unless you're performing a certain role, specifically extraction , support or distracting. Extraction toons unless your super slow should always have extraction trinkets to help aid them in finishing floors faster. If you're a support, it's usually in your best to bring a cool down trinket if you have an important role. Though if your cool down is short you should bring an extraction and speed trinket mix. For distractors there's 4 main combos you should use but no matter what you should always have at minimum a walk speed trinket. As all distractor toons can outwalk all twisteds with that.
